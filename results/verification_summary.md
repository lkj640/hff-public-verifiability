# Automated verification of AI-drafted assessments
Source: website snapshot of 15 September 2026; PubMed/PMC records retrieved 2026-11-05.
Scope: papers cited for the 406 scored ingredients (872 citations, 670 unique PMIDs).

## (a) Citation existence and bibliographic match
- PMID resolves in PubMed: 870/872 (99.8%)
- PMID missing (no PMID recorded): 2
- PMID resolves to a textbook chapter (StatPearls), not a study: 4 citations for 4 ingredients
- Title matches (similarity ≥ 0.85): 861/870 (99.0%)
- Title similarity < 0.85: 9 (on inspection, all are the same article: trademark symbols such as ® rendered as (R), or titles shortened with '...')
- Journal matches: 870/870 (100.0%)
- Year exact: 866/870 (99.5%); within 1 year: 2; mismatch: 2

## (b) Numbers in the rationales found in title + abstract
- All scored: 1394 numbers checked, 1167/1394 (83.7%) found in the PubMed record; citations with ≥ 1 number not found: 188/870 (21.6%)
- Full text used: 195 numbers checked, 139/195 (71.3%) found in the PubMed record; citations with ≥ 1 number not found: 26/78 (33.3%)
- Abstract-level: 1199 numbers checked, 1028/1199 (85.7%) found in the PubMed record; citations with ≥ 1 number not found: 162/792 (20.5%)
- Manual inspection of a random sample showed that many numbers not found are derived from the abstract (e.g., a total sample size computed from group sizes: 19 = 11 smokers + 8 non-smokers), so the share of genuinely unsupported numbers is lower than these figures; each flagged citation still needs manual checking.
- Numbers not found are not necessarily wrong (they may come from the full text or be derived, e.g. rounded or computed); they are the list for manual checking.
- Citations without an abstract in PubMed: 1

- These ingredients need their funding grade re-assessed from the full text; the remaining grade-3 ingredients without open-access full text need manual retrieval.
