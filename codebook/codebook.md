# Scoring codebook: investigator-developed descriptive verifiability index

Each of seven domains is graded 1–5. Points = grade / 5 × domain weight; the composite score is the sum of points (maximum 100). The point value of each grade is given in parentheses. Anchors are translated from the Korean criteria published on the author's evidence website (version of 15 September 2026).

| Domain (weight) | Grade 1 | Grade 2 | Grade 3 | Grade 4 | Grade 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Effect size & statistical rigor (20) | Non-significant, or effect size not reported (4) | Statistically significant but the actual difference is very small (8) | Small but perceptible significant effect (12) | Moderate effect size reported (16) | Consistently large and clear effects across multiple endpoints (20) |
| Study design (15) | No human study (animal or cell studies only) (0–3) | Trial registered, results not yet available (6) | Randomized controlled comparison (9) | Double-blind randomized controlled trial (12) | Double-blind RCT that is also officially registered and multicentre (15) |
| Participants & attrition (15) | < 30 participants (3) | 30–49 participants (6) | 50–79 participants (9) | 80–129 participants (12) | ≥ 130 participants (15) |
| Journal standing (10) | Publication venue cannot be identified (2) | Low-credibility venue, or preprint before formal peer review (4) | Peer-reviewed academic journal (6) | Well-cited, trusted journal (8) | Systematic review or meta-analysis (10) |
| Replication (15) | Single study, never repeated (3) | Studies of similar materials exist, but this product has not been re-tested (6) | Partially consistent results with similar strains or materials (9) | Meta-analysis available, or ≥ 2 independent trials with consistent results (12) | Consistent results replicated across multiple countries and institutions (15) |
| Funding independence (15) | Several authors affiliated with the manufacturer, or fully sponsored (3) | Manufacturer stated as providing funding or test material (6) | Funding source not clearly identified (9) | Independent funding, but no conflict-of-interest statement (12) | Government or academic funding with an explicit declaration of no conflict of interest (15) |
| Safety & adverse events (10) | No human safety data (2) | Adverse-event data not detailed (4) | Safety data present, with some signals of concern (6) | Absence of serious adverse events confirmed (8) | Safety repeatedly confirmed across studies (10) |

## Decision rules

- **Retention.** A publication was retained when the test material corresponded to the recognized ingredient and at least one outcome corresponded to the recognized functional claim. Studies of a different material, a different route of administration (e.g., topical use for an oral ingredient), or a non-matching outcome were not retained.
- **Notified ingredients.** Notified ingredients are non-proprietary, so their evidence was assessed from systematic reviews and meta-analyses of the ingredient as a whole rather than from any single applicant's product.
- **Journal standing.** Judged from peer-review status, SCIE/Scopus indexing and academic reputation, not from impact factor alone.
- **Not scored.** When no human study matching the ingredient and its function was retained, the ingredient was not scored and the reason was recorded.

## Binary criteria used in the manuscript

| Criterion | Rule |
|---|---|
| Randomized controlled trial | design ≥ 3 |
| Double-blind RCT | design ≥ 4 |
| ≥ 50 participants | participants ≥ 3 |
| Peer-reviewed publication | journal ≥ 3 |
| Independent replication | replication ≥ 4 |
| No independent replication | replication = 1 |
| Manufacturer-sponsored or -led | funding 1–2 |
| Funding not reported | funding = 3 |
| Independent funding documented | funding 4–5 |
| Single-article evidence base | one retained article |
